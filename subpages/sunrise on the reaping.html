Sunrise on the Reaping

Plot

Sunrise on the Reaping, was released on March 18, 2025.
The novel is set 24 years before The Hunger Games events, during the 50th Hunger Games won by Haymitch Abernathy The 50th Annual Hunger Games reaping is held on Haymitch's sixteenth birthday. To celebrate this milestone anniversary, dubbed the Second Quarter Quell, all districts must provide four tributes instead of the typical two. At the District 12 reaping, a male tribute, Woodbine Chance, attempts to escape but is shot and killed. In the ensuing chaos, Haymitch protects Lenore Dove from Peacekeepers.The other District 12 tributes include 18-year-old betting oddsmaker Wyatt Callow, 13-year-old neighbor and "sweetheart" Louella McCoy, and 16-year-old Maysilee Donner, who has a twin sister, Merrilee.

Capitol media dismisses the District 12 tributes as "long shots" immediately after their arrival. During the Tribute Parade, a horse-drawn chariot accident kills Louella. Haymitch defiantly carries her body down the parade route, lays her in front of the tyrannical ruler of Panem, President Snow,Beetee, a former victor cruelly forced to mentor his own son, Ampert, as punishment for rebellious activities, recruits Haymitch into a covert plan to sabotage the arena.

The Games commence in a stunningly beautiful arena. Haymitch abandons the Newcomer alliance to carry out his sabotage plan and obtains a backpack filled with charcoal tablets, water, hard-boiled eggs, and potatoes. Haymitch drinks water from a poisonous stream but saves himself with charcoal tablets. Thus, he deduces that he should not trust any food except that supplied by the Capitol. Wyatt is killed on the first day while protecting Lou Lou, who briefly finds her way to Haymitch but is killed by inhaling toxic pollen. Haymitch reunites with Ampert after Lou Lou's death and enters an access channel designed to release genetically mutated creatures ("mutts"). Haymitch triggers the explosives while Ampert guards the exit; this floods the arena's subterranean channels. However, despite briefly promising technical malfunctions, the flood fails to disable the arena's computer systems. Haymitch returns to the arena's surface to find a pack of carnivorous squirrel mutts swarming Ampert and devouring him until only his skeleton remains.

Determined to find another way to destroy the arena, Haymitch convinces Maysilee to explore its perimeter. During another fight with Careers, the tributes surprise a trio of young Gamemakers performing maintenance. Maysilee and a District 4 Career, Maritte, kill them before fleeing. Haymitch and Maysilee find a power generator at the arena's edge, and Haymitch discovers the generator's protective force field which repels objects thrown at it. After Haymitch tries to save her life by spurning her company, Maysilee is killed by waterbird mutts which Haymitch realizes were sent to punish her for killing the Gamemakers. Mortally wounded, Haymitch detonates the final explosive against the force field in a last-ditch attempt to destroy the arena, but realizes it remains intact even as he loses consciousness, believing himself dying.

Upon returning to District 12, Haymitch discovers his home burned and his mother and brother murdered by the fire. Later, his reunion with Lenore Dove in a quiet meadow turns tragic when Haymitch unknowingly feeds her a poisoned gumdrop, an act orchestrated by President Snow. Lenore Dove dies in his arms, imploring him to prevent another "sunrise on the reaping." Haunted by his losses, Haymitch retreats into isolation and alcoholism in the Victors' Village, distancing himself from former friends.



Film Adaptation

The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping premiered in London on October 28, 2026, and was theatrically released in the United States by Lionsgate on November 20, 2026. The film received mixed reviews from critics, and has grossed $56 million worldwide with a budget of $150 million.
